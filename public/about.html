Co-Founder & Matchmaking AdvisorGopi Shah

Gopi Shah brings hands-on matrimonial service and decades of community leadership to HeartLink.

Read Gopi's story

At HeartLink, Gopi Shah brings a hands-on, family-first approach to every conversation. She believes matchmaking is not about biodatas, but about understanding families.

Previously, she founded and ran Jeevansaathi, an honorary matrimonial service in Juhu. Built entirely on trust and word-of-mouth, she worked directly with families to make thoughtful introductions.

Her commitment to community goes back decades. Her work has included promoting education at the Khar Education Society, collaborating to showcase Indian culture in India and the United States, and initiatives supporting tribal communities with NGO Jwala.

She serves as the Maharashtra Chair of the G100 International Textile Women Wing and is a recipient of the prestigious Dr. Sarojini Naidu International Award.

Co-Founder & Relationship AdvisorAlpa Apoorva

Alpa Apoorva helps individuals and families look beyond biodata to readiness, expectations and long-term fit.

Read Alpa's story

Alpa Apoorva is a certified and renowned emotional therapist, relationship coach, transformation coach, and menopause trainer. Her 100% success rate speaks to her compassionate, patient, and non-judgmental approach, ensuring every individual feels genuinely heard and valued.

She helps individuals discover their authentic selves, cultivating organic connections between soulmates and uniting families.

Her expertise enhances personal growth while strengthening family bonds, fostering a happy, harmonious life through thoughtful guidance and emotional clarity.

By helping people resolve conflicts and build deeper understanding, Alpa supports stronger relationships and helps prevent breakups or divorces.
