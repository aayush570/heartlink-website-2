Ways to work together

Four levels of personal attention.

Each engagement begins with understanding the individual and family. We recommend the right level of support after a private conversation.

01 / An introduction

Private Registry

A discreet, no-fee way to introduce your family to HeartLink. We review the profile privately and make contact only when an active search presents a credible fit. The Registry is a place to be considered, rather than an active search.

02 / Success based

Success Plan

For families seeking selected introductions without an upfront advisory fee. We understand preferences, consider suitable candidates and guide introductions. A fee applies only when a match succeeds, on terms agreed privately.

03 / Retained search

Premium Search

A founder-led search shaped around the individual, family and their priorities. We make discreet enquiries across trusted networks, assess compatibility and present carefully considered possibilities.

04 / Private advisory

Private Circle

Our most personal and discreet engagement. Both founders stay closely involved, with a wider trusted search, carefully controlled profile visibility and guidance through important family conversations.

Crafting connections · Seven steps

Considered at every stage.

Our guidance extends beyond profiles to the people and families building a life together.

  1. 01

    Personalised partner selection

    We learn each person’s personality, priorities, preferences and family dynamics before shaping the search.

  2. 02

    Considered profile presentation

    We present carefully selected profiles with attention to emotional, intellectual and social compatibility.

  3. 03

    Compatibility reassessment

    When two people connect, we revisit their priorities and see how compatibility develops in conversation.

  4. 04

    Courtship guidance

    We help couples speak openly about needs, expectations and whether their lives can move forward together.

  5. 05

    Premarital preparation

    We discuss rights, responsibilities and expectations with both individuals before marriage.

  6. 06

    Guidance for both families

    We support communication between the families through decisions and wedding planning.

  7. 07

    Support after marriage

    Where agreed, guidance can continue for up to a year as the couple adjusts and grows together.
