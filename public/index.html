Privacy, by design

Your details stay private.

Last updated 11 August 2026. HeartLink handles enquiry information for private matchmaking, review and follow-up.

What we collect

Contact details and, depending on the enquiry, information about identity, age, location, education, work, family, preferences, social links, relationship context and basic technical submission data.

Why we use it

To review and verify enquiries, understand whether we can help, maintain the Private Registry, consider relevant introductions and provide agreed services.

How we share it

Access within HeartLink is limited to people involved in review or service. A prospective match receives identifying information only after approval and genuine mutual interest. Confidential service providers may support secure storage and site operations, and information may be disclosed when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Retention & security

We keep information while it is relevant to the Registry, an active engagement, legitimate follow-up or a legal requirement, and use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.

Your choices

You may ask for access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent or raise a concern. We may verify identity or authority first. Please do not submit information about a minor.

Privacy contact

For a privacy request or complaint, contact privacy@heartlink.in. If you enquire for another adult, you must be authorised to share their details.
